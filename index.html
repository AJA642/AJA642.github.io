Featured Project

Performance vs PrestigeBias in Football Player Valuations

Python / pandas / XGBoost / LightGBM / SHAP / Streamlit

An interactive scouting dashboard and bias-detection study asking whether football players are valued for their performance — or for the league they happen to play in.

MSc dissertation project, University of Birmingham Dubai. Built on 2,061 players across Europe's Big 5 leagues for the 2024-25 season, using position-specific models and SHAP analysis to separate a player's own output from their league's effect on their market value.
