Featured Project

Performance vs Prestige: Detecting Bias in Football Player Valuations

An interactive scouting dashboard and bias-detection study asking whether players are valued for their performance — or for the league they happen to play in.

MSc dissertation project, University of Birmingham Dubai. Built on 2,061 players across Europe's Big 5 leagues for the 2024-25 season, combining performance data from FBref with market valuations from Transfermarkt.

Position-specific models — XGBoost, LightGBM, and Ridge — predict each player's market value from their on-pitch performance. SHAP analysis then breaks that prediction apart to show how much of it is explained by the player's own output versus their league or confederation. The result is a 5-page Streamlit dashboard — Home, Model Performance, Value Finder, Player Explorer, Bias Explorer — built for scouting and exploring the data, not just reading about it.
